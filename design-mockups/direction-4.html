Direction 4 of 5 · Retro Diner

The Regular

The 1962 counter diner, reborn as an app. Cherry red and malt cream, checkerboard trim, badges that look hand-lettered on the window. Orders print like kitchen tickets, pickup times come on enamel pins. Warm, loud, and impossible to mistake for another coffee app — you’re not a user, you’re a regular.

Logo

A roadside diner badge: marquee-bulb ring, tilted “Break!” and a checker counter strip. The oval crops to a circle for the app icon and still reads at a glance.
