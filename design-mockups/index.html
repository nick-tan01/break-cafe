BREAK · Brand Exploration · June 2026

Five ways to take a break. Pick the one that feels like us.

BREAK lets students and young professionals order ahead at LA’s independent cafes and walk past the line. Each direction below is a complete visual identity — logo, palette, type system — applied to the three core screens (Explore, cafe menu, checkout) at full iPhone fidelity. They are deliberately far apart; pick a lane, then we refine.

Brand ideaSkip the line, savor the moment
AudienceStudents & young professionals, LA
Screens shownExplore · Menu · Checkout

6 · Daybreak

Your pick — hybrid
#E7EFF8 #232B3A #4F8268 #A9C6E8 #CDC3EC

After Hours’ bones, Cloud Cover’s light. Direction 5’s letterspaced Marcellus, hairline rules and menu-board composition, re-lit with Direction 3’s morning-sky gradient, frosted glass and single sage accent. Gold hairlines become sage on glass; the latte-ring mark stays, its crema crescent flipped east into a sunrise. Quietly upscale, fully awake.

1 · Golden Hour

Warm Editorial
#FBF4E6 #3B2A1D #C75B39 #E2A75E #6F7048

The neighborhood cafe at 7:40 AM, before the rush. Cream paper, terracotta ink and Fraunces serif headlines make ordering ahead feel like a small daily ritual rather than a transaction. The sunrise-over-cup mark literalizes “golden hour.” Safest fit for third-wave cafe partners — it flatters their counters and their pastry case.

2 · Heavy Pour

Bold Brutalist
#F4F1E7 #141414 #D8F34F #FF5A1F #B9B6AC

Coffee for people moving fast. Near-black on acid yellow, hard borders, shadows that don’t blur, Archivo Black shouting SKIP THE LINE. The wordmark is literally cracked in two. Loudest shelf presence of the five and instantly screenshot-able — built for a student brand that spreads by being seen.

Archivo Black + Space Grotesk 400–700 View direction

3 · Cloud Cover

Soft Modern iOS
#E7EFF8 #232B3A #4F8268 #A9C6E8 #CDC3EC

A breather, rendered natively. Frosted glass over a morning-sky gradient, one calm sage accent, deep corner radii, a single type family. The mark is the universal pause symbol — press pause, take a break. Feels like a first-party Apple app; the lowest-friction choice for daily use and the easiest to build well in React Native.

Manrope 400–800 (single family) View direction

4 · The Regular

Playful Retro Diner
#FFF4DF #D62F2F #3E8E72 #F2B33D #221F1A

The 1962 counter diner, reborn as an app. Cherry red and malt cream, checker trim, marquee-bulb badges, orders that print like kitchen tickets numbered № 1147. The warmest personality of the five — you’re not a user, you’re a regular. Strongest merch and sticker potential; the riskiest if partner cafes skew minimalist.

Fugaz One + Rubik 400–700 View direction

5 · After Hours

Premium Dark
#0F0C09 #15110C #D5A04F #A9683A #EFE3CF

The espresso bar at 9 PM. Near-black roast surfaces, crema-gold hairlines, a letterspaced serif set like the menu board behind the bar. The mark is a latte from above, its crema forming a crescent moon — last cup of the night. Owns the “open late” use case nobody else designs for, and reads the most premium of the five.
